How much does a bike service cost?

A basic service costs 45 pounds and a full service costs 85 pounds. Parts are charged at cost. We quote before we start, so the price you are given is the price you pay.

Do you collect bikes from home?

Yes. We collect and return within five miles of the shop for a flat 10 pounds, and most bikes are back with you the same day.
