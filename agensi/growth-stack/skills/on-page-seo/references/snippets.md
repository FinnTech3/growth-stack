# Title and description patterns

Lengths are rules of thumb: engines truncate by pixel width, so 30 to 60 characters usually shows in full.

## Titles

| Pattern | Example (fictional) |
|---|---|
| Topic, then qualifier, then brand | Bike repair in Harbour Street, same-day service \| Harbour Street Bikes |
| Question the page answers | How much does a bike service cost? Fixed prices from 45 pounds |
| Offer with a limit | Brake and gear service, 90-day guarantee |

Avoid: the brand first on every page, "Home" or "Welcome", all caps, the same title on two pages.

## Descriptions

Formula: what the visitor gets, one proof point from the page, one action. 70 to 160 characters.

- Good: "Same-day bike repair in Harbour Street. Fixed prices, free quotes and a 90-day guarantee on every job. Book online."
- Weak: "Welcome to our website. We offer a wide range of services." (no promise, no proof, no action)

## Headings

- One H1 states the topic. H2s are the questions people ask, in the order they ask them.
- Never use a heading for styling. Never skip from H2 to H4.

## Share tags

`og:title` and `og:description` can be shorter and warmer than the search ones. `og:image` needs an absolute URL.
