# Intent modifiers

A rule of thumb for labelling. Read the keyword, then the page that would satisfy it.

| Intent | Typical words | Page that fits |
|---|---|---|
| Informational | how, what, why, guide, tutorial, examples, meaning, vs (neutral comparison) | Guide, explainer, FAQ |
| Commercial | best, top, review, compare, alternatives, pricing (research stage) | Comparison, review, buyer's guide |
| Transactional | buy, order, book, quote, hire, price, near me, discount | Product, service or booking page |
| Navigational | a brand name, login, contact, address, opening times | The brand's own page |

## Edge calls

- "Cost" and "price" can be commercial (comparing) or transactional (ready to buy). Judge by the other words.
- "Near me" and a place name are transactional with local intent: route to `local-seo`.
- A question that ends in a product name is informational until the user shows otherwise.

## Page types

Guide, comparison, product or service page, category page, tool or calculator, FAQ, location page. One type per cluster.
