# Answer patterns

Lengths are rules of thumb. A short answer is easier to lift cleanly; a long one is easier to misquote.

## The shape

1. The question, as a heading, in the words a buyer uses.
2. The answer in one paragraph of about 40 to 60 words: the direct answer first, then the reason.
3. Detail below: a list for steps, a table for specs, a link to the source.

## Before and after (fictional)

Before: "Our servicing packages are designed to deliver a comprehensive solution tailored to your needs."

After, under the heading "How much does a bike service cost?": "A basic service costs 45 pounds and covers brakes, gears,
tyres and a safety check. Parts are charged at cost. We quote before we start, so the price you are given is the price you pay."

## Entities

State who, what, where and for whom in the first paragraph, and use the same names in the title, markup and footer.
Link the organisation to its real profiles with `sameAs`.

## Sources

A claim with a number needs a link to where the number came from. If it came from the user's own records, say
"our records, March 2025" and keep the date. If there is no source, remove the number.

## Crawl access

The decision to allow or block each crawler belongs to the site owner. The probe reports the rule that decides it.
