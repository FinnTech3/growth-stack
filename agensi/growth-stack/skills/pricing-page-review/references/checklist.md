# Pricing page checklist

## Structure

- [ ] Three or four plans (rule of thumb), or a clear chooser if more.
- [ ] Names describe the buyer.
- [ ] One recommended plan, visually distinct.
- [ ] Same button label on every plan, describing the next step.

## Each plan

- [ ] Price, currency, billing period.
- [ ] Included items ordered by what the buyer values, with limits as numbers.
- [ ] Who it is for and who it is not for.
- [ ] What happens at the limit (stops, overage price, upgrade prompt).

## Money

- [ ] Tax treatment stated (including or excluding VAT or sales tax).
- [ ] All fees shown before checkout.
- [ ] Annual versus monthly: show both prices and the actual difference.
- [ ] Trial, cancellation and refund terms with exact days and steps.

## Trust

- [ ] Contact route visible as text.
- [ ] Policies linked.
- [ ] Proof the user can show, near the plans.

## Questions to answer on the page

1. Can I switch plans later? 2. What if I go over? 3. Is there a contract? 4. How do I cancel? 5. Who do I ask?

## Dark patterns to report as findings

Pre-ticked extras, a trial that converts without a clear reminder, a cancel path longer than the sign-up path, prices that change at checkout, countdowns that reset.
