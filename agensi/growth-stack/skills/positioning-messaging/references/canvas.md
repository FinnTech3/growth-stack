## Statement

For [customer] who [need], [product] is a [category] that [value]. Unlike [alternative], it [difference], shown by [proof].

## Message hierarchy

1. Headline claim (one sentence, the visitor's outcome).
2. Support 1, with proof. 3. Support 2, with proof. 4. Support 3, with proof.
5. Objections and the honest answer to each (price, switching, risk, trust, fit).
